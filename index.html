Calculate what you need on your final exam.

Enter your target grade, any extra credit, and the weights of your assignments. You can choose to input either total category weights or individual assignment weights.

NOTE: The extra credit will be added as a bonus % to your current grade. (Current Grade (%) + Extra Credit (%) = Adjusted Current Grade) Your final exam threshold does include the extra credit. If extra credit is per assignment and not an overall bonus, simply add it to the assignment score. Example: +10% to an 100% assignment would be 110% score for that assignment.
